# EPV Synthesis Benchmark v0.1

The machine-readable M6 corpus contains five finite auction problems: unoptimized synthesis, exact revenue optimization, payment-only repair, bounded UNSAT, and reserve synthesis. Every case declares its variables, bounds, requirements, objective, search size, expected status, and exhaustive reference method.

The benchmark intentionally remains tiny. Exhaustive enumeration is used as the optimality and minimality oracle. No result generalizes beyond its declared table or parameter family.
