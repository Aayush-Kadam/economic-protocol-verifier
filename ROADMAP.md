# Roadmap

1. M0 — scientific definition and prior art: complete, narrowed.
2. M1 — immutable typed kernel plus exhaustive finite oracle.
3. M2 — small EPL surface syntax, semantic validation, canonical serialization, and one SMT backend.
4. M3 — replayable and minimized counterexamples.
5. M4 — experimental certificate path limited to theories actually accepted by an independent checker.
6. M5–M8 — proceed only after earlier gates; cross-domain breadth, synthesis, UI, and paper are not commitments.

The initial research slice is deterministic direct-revelation, finite exact domains, quasi-linear utility, explicit deterministic tie-breaking, and four properties: DSIC, ex-post IR, weak budget balance, and single-resource feasibility.
