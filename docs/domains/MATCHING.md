# Matching

M5 matching is one-to-one, two-sided matching with strict complete rankings over every counterpart plus `unmatched`. Rankings are ordinal; there are no numerical utilities. Outcomes map each proposer to at most one receiver, and receiver assignments are derived symmetrically.

The canonical mechanism is proposer deferred acceptance. Free proposers apply in reported order; a receiver holds its most-preferred acceptable proposal; rejected proposers continue. Iteration order is proposer index order. Properties are feasibility, individual rationality, stability, and proposer strategy-proofness on the explicitly enumerated 2x2 report domain.

A blocking-pair witness records both agents, current partners, and both strict preference comparisons. The fixed-matching mutant supplies a replayable negative stability case.
