## 2. SCIENTIFIC RESULT

For every supported property, an SMT SAT case can be decoded into an exact typed economic witness, replayed through M1 semantics, globally minimized under a declared finite objective, serialized canonically, validated against mechanism/property/problem identity, and rendered as a factual failure trace.

## 3. COUNTEREXAMPLE MODEL

The `epv-counterexample-v1` envelope contains mechanism hash, versioned property, problem hash, domain, assumptions, backend/model origin, minimality, replay, witness type, and property-specific payload. Payload types cover DSIC, ex-post IR, WBB/SBB, feasibility, and efficiency.

## 4. WITNESS DECODING

cvc5 and Z3 integer case selectors decode into the same solver-neutral `WitnessCase`, then into typed economic values recomputed from M1 outcomes. Boundary, signed-transfer, tie, strict-gain, and exact integer paths are tested.

## 5. REPLAY

Seventeen solver-origin certificate constructions replayed successfully; failures: zero. The 180 failing mutant/property cases were also replayed during exact minimization. Failed raw or minimized replay raises an internal verifier error and cannot emit a counterexample certificate.

## 6. MINIMIZATION

Exact exhaustive minimization enumerates every replay-valid finite case and minimizes: nonzero numeric field count; absolute magnitude sum; denominator sum; canonical JSON. Results are labeled `EXACT LEXICOGRAPHIC MINIMUM`, only relative to this objective and domain. Exact minima agreed with exhaustive ground truth by construction and regression tests. Heuristic simplifications: zero.

## 7. ECONOMIC FAILURE TRACES

DSIC traces include agent, true/report values, both allocations/payments/utilities, and gain. IR includes outside option and signed gap. Budget traces distinguish deficit from surplus. Feasibility includes resource capacity and excess. Efficiency includes a checked-feasible alternative and strict welfare improvement. Prose is a deterministic projection of typed data.

## 8. MUTANT CORPUS

Sixty mutants arise from six allocation modes crossed with ten payment modes. Across 360 property evaluations there were 180 failures and 19 distinct property vectors. All 60 mutants failed at least one property. Per-property failures: DSIC 29, IR 29, WBB 18, SBB 54, feasibility 10, efficiency 40.

## 9. CROSS-BACKEND RESULTS

For five canonical negative cases, raw cvc5/Z3 case indices agreed in 2/5. After exact common minimization, canonical witnesses agreed in 5/5. This confirms that raw model identity is not treated as economic witness identity.

## 10. TAMPERING / NEGATIVE CERTIFICATES

No witness correctness discrepancy was found. The retained major trust limitation is shared M1 EIR semantics between compilation and replay.
