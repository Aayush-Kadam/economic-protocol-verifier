# Property registry

The normative machine-readable registry is `property-registry.json`. It freezes 14 property families: six auction properties (DSIC, ex-post IR, weak and strong budget balance, feasibility, allocative efficiency), four matching properties (feasibility, individual rationality, stability, proposer strategyproofness), and four fair-division properties (feasibility, envy-freeness, EF1, Pareto efficiency).

Every entry records its immutable identifier/version, mathematical meaning, applicability, assumptions, verification method, maximum demonstrated assurance, and limitation. “Maximum” means demonstrated for at least one committed instance, not available for every input.
