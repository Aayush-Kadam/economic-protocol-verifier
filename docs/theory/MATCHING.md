# Matching

EPV models one-to-one two-sided markets with strict complete ordinal rankings, including an unmatched outside option. Deferred acceptance and fixed-rule examples produce deterministic matchings.

Feasibility requires consistent one-to-one partners. Individual rationality requires every assigned partner to rank above unmatched for both participants. Stability excludes a cross-side pair whose members each strictly prefer the other to their current assignment. Proposer strategyproofness ranges over the committed finite 2x2 report universe and compares outcomes using true ordinal rankings, never invented cardinal utilities.

Ties, incomplete rankings, capacities, couples, transfers, and large markets are outside the fragment.
