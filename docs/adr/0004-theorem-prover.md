# ADR 0004: Theorem prover

Status: deferred, Lean experiment preferred.

Isabelle has the strongest direct auction and SMT-reconstruction precedent; Coq has `mech.v`; Lean 4 has an active ecosystem and a small kernel. EPV will not maintain three integrations. M4 may attempt one narrow Lean 4 reconstruction after pinning a fixed release and reviewing its soundness notes. Failure returns the decision to deferred status.
