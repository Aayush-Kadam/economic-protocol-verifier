# Synthesis attacks

All 32 subsets of five auction requirements were searched in the 16-candidate class and independently revalidated. Tampered result hashes, malformed priors, timeout-to-UNSAT, fake nonminimal cores, changed search bounds, and out-of-class repair baselines were rejected.

Exhaustive enumeration reconfirmed candidate status, canonical tie-break, objective `1/2`, repair distance `(1,1)`, reserve optimum `(2,10/9)`, and bounded-class UNSAT.
