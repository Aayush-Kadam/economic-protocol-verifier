# Failure Traces

Traces are deterministic projections of validated typed witnesses. DSIC reports both report paths, allocations, payments, utilities, and strict gain. IR reports utility, outside option, and signed gap. WBB/SBB report ordered transfers, net balance, magnitude, and whether the balance is a deficit or surplus. Feasibility reports resource, capacity, allocation, total, and excess. Efficiency reports the chosen and strictly superior feasible allocation with exact welfare values.

The renderer makes no causal claim beyond these calculations. Rendering does not establish truth; successful formal replay does.
